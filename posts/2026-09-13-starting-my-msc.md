After graduating with First Class Honours in Personalised Medicine, I'm starting the next stage of my studies with an MSc in Biotechnology Research.

## Why biotechnology?

My interest in biotechnology comes from wanting to understand how biological research, technology and data can be combined to answer complex questions.

## What I'm hoping to learn

During my MSc, I'm hoping to develop my:

- Research skills
- Laboratory and biotechnology knowledge
- Bioinformatics skills
- Data analysis and interpretation
- Understanding of experimental design

## What I'll be working on

I'll be using this blog to document some of the things I learn throughout my MSc, as well as projects, research papers and concepts that I find interesting.

This is the beginning of a new stage in my academic and research journey.
